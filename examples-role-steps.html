How the contract derives a role's text (the role as text on a surface or its own tint) and its hover / active fills. Today (left) they mix the fill with black (light mode) or white (dark) in sRGB, which darkens and greys the color — sky blue text turns teal-grey. The OKLCH columns lower (or raise) only the lightness and keep hue and chroma. Each badge is tint + role text + the fill button (resting / hover / active); numbers are the text on its tint and on the surface (WCAG 2 ratio · APCA Lc). Status roles' text is a hand-set input in the contract, so only primary / secondary text changes between columns; hover / active change for every role in the last column. Light / dark follows the page's theme switch (top right).
