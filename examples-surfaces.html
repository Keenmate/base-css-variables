Surfaces form a ladder: page < subtle < main < elevated. Hover, active and disabled are a separate axis: translucent overlays that work on any step. A theme sets --base-page-bg, --base-main-bg and --base-text-color-1; the other steps, text levels 2–4 and the border are derived from them. Role surfaces (--base-input-bg, --base-dropdown-bg, --base-tooltip-bg) default into the ladder but can be retargeted alone.

SU01 · The elevation ladder

Each step nested in the one below it. The interaction states are overlays, so the same token works on every step; hover and press the last chip on each surface.

--base-page-bg · the app background
--base-subtle-bg · recessed: code, wells, sidebars
--base-main-bg · cards, panels, the content area
--base-elevated-bg · raised: headers, toolbars, popovers

SU02 · Four text levels

Level 1 is the input; 2–4 are level 1 mixed toward --base-main-bg (75 %, 55 %, 40 %), so they keep their steps in any theme and mode.
