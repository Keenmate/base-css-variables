Every role (primary, secondary, success, danger, warning, info) has the same seven tokens. A theme sets --base-<role>-color (and, for the status roles, the text colour); hover, pressed, the tint and the text on the fill are formulas over it. "Danger, one step darker" is then the same answer in pure-admin and in every component. Per-state button tokens are a component's own design and stay in the component (--pa-btn-*), defaulting to these.

RO01 · The seven tokens

Hover over the fill cells to see the hover step; the text on each fill is picked by APCA contrast (black or white), so a light fill like warning gets dark text.

Role -color-color-hover-color-active -bg-subtle-bg-subtle-hover -text-color--base-on-*-text-color

RO02 · In use

Buttons (solid, soft, outline, disabled), alerts, badges and links, all built from the role tokens. Hover and press the buttons.

RO03 · Change a role colour

Each picker sets one --base-<role>-color on :root. Everything above follows: steps, tints, and the text on the fill flips when the fill gets light or dark enough. Try a pale yellow or a dark navy.
